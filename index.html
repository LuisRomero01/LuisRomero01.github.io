Blog Post One

My best "in class" computing experience has to be working on the projects for my AI class. I consider this one of my favorite experiences because it made me feel like an actual scientist. I was creating charts, comparing results with peers, and adjusting models to get the best results. Although the class was a bit tedious, I still enjoy the stress of having a neural networks taking forever to load because my laptop only had 8gb of RAM at the time.

My best "outside of class" experience is participating in the 2026 Hackathon at App. This was probaly one of the first times I've ever felt like a software developer. My friends and I created a webservice app that made it easy for students, faculity, and visitors to find their lost items by having a website they can go to that tracks lost items turned into buildings. I got to work on the backend side of the project and I really enjoyed planning what frameworks, databases, and features we were going to use. Luckily, we ended up winning the "Most practicle" and got some nerds clusters for a prize.
